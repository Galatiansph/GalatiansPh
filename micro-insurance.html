Frequently asked questions

What is embedded insurance?

It is insurance offered inside another product or service, such as a checkout page or an app, so customers can get covered without a separate sign-up process.

Do I need technical integration?

Some programs are simple enough to run without one, and others connect to your platform. We discuss what works for you during planning.

What kinds of businesses is this for?

Platforms, apps and programs with an existing customer base, such as e-commerce, fintech and membership groups.
