Frequently asked questions

What does motor insurance cover?

Coverage depends on the policy. It can include damage to your own vehicle, third-party liability and other add-ons. We explain what is and is not included before you decide.

Do I need fire insurance for my business?

Fire and property insurance helps you recover after covered damage to your premises, stock or equipment. Lenders and landlords may also require it.

Can you cover a company fleet?

Yes, fleet options are available. Send us the number and type of vehicles in your inquiry.
