Frequently asked questions

Do you offer HMO plans for individuals and families?

Yes. Besides corporate plans for companies, we can help you find an individual or family plan. Available plans, age limits and benefits depend on the plan, so tell us who needs coverage and we will prepare your options.

What does an HMO plan usually cover?

Most HMO plans cover inpatient and outpatient care, emergency care and annual check-ups. Exact benefits, limits, waiting periods and exclusions depend on the plan you choose, and we go through them with you before you decide.

Can I include my family or my employees' dependents?

Many plans allow dependents or family members to be added. Availability and cost depend on the plan, so mention it in your inquiry and we will include it in your options.

Is there a minimum number of employees for a company plan?

Requirements differ by plan. Send us your headcount and we will tell you which options are open to you.
