What is included

  • Annual physical exam (medical history, chest X-ray, urinalysis, fecalysis, CBC)
  • Preventive care, counselling and a wellness program
  • Outpatient consultations, eye, ear, nose and throat care, minor injuries, labs and diagnostics
  • Inpatient and emergency care
  • Telemedicine, which does not reduce your benefit limit
  • COVID-19 treatment within set limits
  • Corporate HR web portal: add or remove members, upload enrollment files, billing and utilization reports
  • Member mobile app with digital card, letter of authorization requests and reimbursement claims

Add-ons and conditions

  • Optional Open Door Dental benefits.
  • ₱10,000 group term life insurance with accidental death and dismemberment for employees, not available for employees aged 65 and above.
  • Pre-existing conditions are covered for employees up to the limit, when all eligible employees are enrolled.
  • Company-paid for employees (all eligible regular employees aged 18 to 65 enrolled). Dependent rates assume at least 75% participation.
  • Members who are not PhilHealth members pay an additional fee and must be declared at enrollment.
  • Pay annually, semi-annually or quarterly. Rates include VAT.

Premiums depend on your access option, limit, room type and headcount. Ask us for your quote. Acceptance is subject to iCare evaluation and documents such as the member list, business permit and BIR registration.

What is covered

Annual physical exam and preventive care

Yearly check-up and preventive services for enrolled employees.

Outpatient care

Consultations, ENT and eye care, minor injuries, minor surgery and laboratory tests.

Inpatient care

Room and board, operating room, ICU and professional fees, up to the maximum benefit limit for the room type you choose.

Emergency care

Cashless in affiliated hospitals, subject to the limit. Non-affiliated or overseas emergencies are reimbursed up to ₱30,000 per case per member per year. Ambulance included.

Special modalities

Dialysis, chemotherapy, radiotherapy and other special treatments, within the limit.

Pre-existing conditions

Covered for principals, subject to the limit. For dependents it depends on participation, see below.

Other benefits

Work-related and motor vehicle accident care, congenital conditions up to ₱60,000, and maternity assistance of ₱5,000 (reimbursable, 280-day waiting period).

Covering dependents

Dependent pre-existing condition cover depends on how many principals enroll a dependent:

  • 75% or more of principals enroll a dependent: pre-existing conditions covered up to the full limit.
  • 50% to 74%: ₱10,000 limit in the first year.
  • Below 50%: 12-month waiting period.

Optional add-ons

Dental

Three packages, from a standard dental plan with oral prophylaxis to packages with extra cleanings and tooth work.

Group Life and accidental death

Coverage amounts from ₱10,000 to ₱100,000 per employee.

Payment can be annual, semi-annual or quarterly.
