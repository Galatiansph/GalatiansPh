Individual plans

Compare iCare Persona, Select and Blue Royale

Three ways to cover yourself or your family. They are built differently, so the best choice depends on whether you want a network-based HMO with outpatient care included, high hospital limits with freedom to choose your doctor, or worldwide cover.

See premiums by age Ask us to compare for you

Covering a team? Group plans have a minimum of 5 enrollees. The plans on this page are for individuals and families. See group plans

iCare Persona

HMO by Insular Health Care

A cashless health-card plan with outpatient, hospital, emergency and preventive care, plus a term life benefit. You choose how wide your hospital network is.

Best for: people who want everyday outpatient care and a predictable, lower cost.

Pacific Cross Select

Health insurance, in pesos

High yearly hospital limits (₱1M to ₱5M) with your own choice of hospital and doctor, cash-free at accredited hospitals or by reimbursement. Travel benefits are included.

Best for: people who want big hospital limits and freedom of choice.

Pacific Cross Blue Royale

Worldwide medical, in US dollars

Limits up to USD 2,000,000 a year with treatment anywhere in the world, plus maternity (Plans B and C) and worldwide emergency assistance.

Best for: frequent travelers, people who want treatment abroad, and growing families.

Read this before comparing prices. The plans are not like for like. Persona's limit applies to each illness or injury and is much lower, but it includes outpatient care and a life benefit. Select and Blue Royale have far higher hospital limits, and outpatient cover is optional or limited.

Which plan fits you?

  • Choose iCare Persona if you want consultations, check-ups and hospital care through one health card, and you are comfortable using accredited providers.
  • Choose Select if your main worry is a large hospital bill and you want to pick your own hospital and doctor. Add outpatient cover if you also want regular consultations.
  • Choose Blue Royale if you travel often, may want treatment abroad, or want maternity cover with a very high limit and can budget in US dollars.

Not sure? Tell us the age and number of people to cover, your budget and any health conditions, and we will compare for you.
